Internal Mobility Guidelines

To ensure smooth transitions and balance across operational teams, candidates must meet the following criteria:

  • Minimum 6 months of tenure in current role
  • Good standing with latest performance review
  • Fulfillment of core technical skills outlined in job specifications
  • Open communication with current manager upon shortlisting
